AI-OPS STORE SUPPORT KNOWLEDGE BASE (SIMULATION)
Last Updated: 2026-04-01

=================================================================
1) ORDER STATUS DEFINITIONS
=================================================================
- Order Confirmed: Payment authorization completed and order accepted.
- Processing: Order is being prepared in the warehouse.
- Packed: Items are packed and waiting for carrier pickup.
- Shipped: Package has left the warehouse; tracking is active.
- In Transit: Package is moving through the carrier network.
- Out for Delivery: Package is expected to be delivered today.
- Delivered: Carrier marked package as delivered.
- Delivery Attempted: Carrier attempted delivery but could not complete it.
- Delayed: Delivery is delayed due to weather, volume, or route exceptions.
- Returned to Sender: Package is being returned to our warehouse.
- Cancelled: Order was canceled before shipment.

Tracking notes:
- Tracking numbers are sent by email/SMS once status changes to Shipped.
- Tracking updates may take up to 24 hours after first scan.
- If status has not changed for 72+ hours, customer may request investigation.

=================================================================
2) SHIPPING POLICY
=================================================================
Shipping options (simulated):
- Standard Shipping: 3-7 business days
- Expedited Shipping: 2-3 business days
- Priority Shipping: 1-2 business days

Important rules:
- Business days exclude weekends and public holidays.
- Orders placed after 5:00 PM local warehouse time are processed next business day.
- Address changes are not guaranteed once order is Packed or later.
- Signature may be required for high-value orders.

International shipping:
- Delivery estimates vary by destination and customs processing.
- Customs duties/taxes are paid by customer unless stated at checkout.

=================================================================
3) RETURN & REFUND POLICY
=================================================================
Eligibility windows:
- Standard items: Return within 30 days of delivery.
- Electronics: Return within 15 days of delivery.
- Apparel/footwear: Return within 30 days if unworn and tags attached.

Non-returnable items:
- Final Sale items
- Gift cards
- Personal care items once opened
- Customized/personalized products

Condition requirements:
- Item must be in original condition and packaging.
- Include all accessories/manuals for electronics.
- Missing components may reduce refund amount.

Fees:
- Electronics may include 15% restocking fee if opened and not defective.
- Return shipping fee may be deducted when prepaid label is used (region-dependent).

Refund timing:
- Refund is issued to original payment method.
- Processing: 3-5 business days after return is received and inspected.
- Bank/card posting time: additional 2-7 business days.

Exchange policy:
- Exchanges are supported for size/color when inventory is available.
- If unavailable, customer can choose refund or store credit.

=================================================================
4) CANCELLATION POLICY
=================================================================
- Cancellation is possible only before shipment.
- If status is Order Confirmed or Processing, cancellation is usually allowed.
- If status is Packed/Shipped/In Transit, cancellation is not possible.
- Customer should refuse delivery or initiate return after delivery if needed.

=================================================================
5) DAMAGED, MISSING, OR WRONG ITEM
=================================================================
Damaged item:
- Report within 7 days of delivery.
- Provide photos of packaging and damaged product.
- Resolution: replacement, refund, or partial refund based on case.

Missing item marked delivered:
- Ask customer to verify delivery location and neighbors.
- Wait up to 24 hours for final carrier drop-off update.
- If still missing, open carrier investigation.

Wrong item received:
- Customer should not use the item.
- Provide order ID and item photos.
- Support arranges replacement or full refund.

=================================================================
6) PAYMENT & BILLING
=================================================================
Accepted payment methods (simulated):
- Credit/Debit cards
- UPI/Net banking
- Wallets
- COD (selected locations)

Common payment states:
- Payment Pending: awaiting gateway confirmation.
- Payment Failed: transaction unsuccessful.
- Refunded: payment reversal initiated/completed.

Duplicate charge handling:
- Temporary authorization holds may appear as duplicate charges.
- Most holds auto-release within 3-5 business days.
- If posted duplicate remains beyond 7 business days, escalate billing ticket.

=================================================================
7) PROMOTIONS, COUPONS, AND PRICE MATCH
=================================================================
- Only one promo code can be applied per order unless campaign says otherwise.
- Promo codes cannot be applied after order placement.
- Expired codes are invalid.
- Price match allowed within 7 days of purchase for eligible products.
- Exclusions: clearance/final sale/limited-time flash deals.

=================================================================
8) WARRANTY SUPPORT (SIMULATED)
=================================================================
- Standard manufacturer warranty applies where applicable.
- Warranty claims require invoice/order proof.
- Physical damage, water damage, and misuse are excluded unless stated by manufacturer.

=================================================================
9) ACCOUNT, PRIVACY, AND SECURITY
=================================================================
- Customers can request account data export/deletion via support verification.
- Address/email/phone updates require identity verification for security.
- Refund destination cannot be changed from original payment source.

=================================================================
10) SUPPORT ESCALATION GUIDELINES
=================================================================
Escalate to specialist queue when:
- Order is stuck In Transit for 72+ hours with no movement.
- High-value package marked Delivered but customer reports non-receipt.
- Repeated payment failures with successful bank debit.
- Customer reports legal/compliance/privacy complaints.

Priority SLA (simulated):
- P1 critical (payment charged + no order): response within 2 hours.
- P2 urgent (missing/damaged high-value): response within 6 hours.
- P3 normal (policy query): response within 24 hours.

=================================================================
11) AGENT RESPONSE STYLE FOR USERS
=================================================================
- Use plain, clear, and empathetic language.
- Confirm the customer concern in one sentence.
- Provide next step with expected timeline.
- Avoid legal or absolute promises unless explicitly in policy.
- If information is unavailable, say so clearly and offer escalation.
